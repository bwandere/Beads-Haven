Our Story

Handmade with pride in Kenya

Amazing Outfits is a celebration of Kenyan beadwork and African artistic heritage, translated into contemporary jewellery, wearable accessories, and home decor.

Every piece in our collection is carefully strung and woven by skilled artisans in Nairobi. From intricate Maasai cuff patterns and cowrie shell chokers to statement beaded bags and wall portraits, our creations carry stories of cultural pride, patience, and meticulous craft.

Whether you are choosing a gift, styling an outfit for a celebration, or commissioning bespoke beadwork for your home or wedding, we bring authentic craftsmanship right to your doorstep anywhere in Kenya.

100% Handcrafted

Every glass seed bead and cowrie shell is individually strung by hand with durable thread and tested finishes.

Local Artisans

We work directly with bead artisans in Nairobi, fostering creative livelihoods and keeping traditional craft thriving.

Custom Commissions

From bespoke bridal sets and family waist beads to corporate gifts, we bring your custom concepts to life.
